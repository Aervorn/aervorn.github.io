The short version: **neither this website nor my apps collect anything about you.** No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.

## This website

This site is a set of static pages. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't run any analytics or tracking script, and doesn't embed content from social networks. Fonts are served by the site itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other third party to display them.

The site is hosted by [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com). Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data when you load a page, such as your IP address, and may keep it for security and operational purposes. I don't have access to it. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## Navloom

Navloom collects nothing and sends nothing about you. It reads your browsers' bookmarks, icons and a few settings (profile names, sync status) on your Mac, and changes them only when you ask.

The only network requests are made by FaviconSync and Ghosts, straight to the websites of your own bookmarks, to fetch their icons or check that they respond. Nothing goes through a server of mine.

## Flux

Flux collects nothing and sends nothing about you. Your feeds, articles, favorites and settings are stored on your Mac.

The only network requests go to the sites you subscribe to: their feeds, article pages, images and icons. If you turn on sync, Flux stores a small file per Mac in your own iCloud Drive, which only you can access.

## ForkWatch

ForkWatch runs on your own server and sends nothing to me. Its data stays in the folder you choose on that server.

It contacts the Git hosts of the repositories you watch to check for changes. If you set them up, it also calls the GitHub API with your token (to sync your forks) and sends notifications to your Discord webhook.

## Downloads and updates

The apps are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub may record the download like any other request. The apps don't check for updates on their own yet.

## Contact

For any question, open an issue on the GitHub repository of the app concerned: [Navloom](https://github.com/Aervorn/MacOS_Navloom/issues), [Flux](https://github.com/Aervorn/MacOS_Flux/issues) or [ForkWatch](https://github.com/Aervorn/Docker_ForkWatch/issues).
