Mole comes in two forms: `mo`, a free and open-source command-line tool, and **Mole for Mac**, a native SwiftUI app sold as a one-time purchase. Both share the same cleanup scope and the same safety rules: whatever Mole can’t prove is safe, it leaves alone and tells you why.
