Thaw started as a fork of Ice, the free menu bar manager that stopped working on macOS 26. It sorts icons into visible, hidden and always-hidden sections, and goes beyond hiding: tint, shadow, border and shape of the bar, separate settings for light and dark mode, and `thaw://` links to drive it from a launcher.
