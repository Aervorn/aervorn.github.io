Vorssaint brings into the menu bar what usually takes half a dozen apps: per-app volume and audio output, CPU, GPU and network stats, Dock window previews, clipboard history, screenshots with text recognition, an uninstaller… Each feature only asks for the macOS permission it needs, when you turn it on.
